      FAQ & install notes

      Will the modified app update the original?

      No. The new package name and a different signing key mean Android treats it as a separate app. Expect a fresh install with no data carried over.

      Can both apps be installed together?

      Often yes, when the application IDs differ. Apps that share fixed identities (accounts, content providers, hardcoded package names) can still conflict.

      Why might the app crash or misbehave?

      Signature checks, licensing, integrity verification (e.g. Play Integrity), hardcoded package names and server-side restrictions can all break after modification.

      Which files are unsupported?

      Split APKs, app bundles (AAB/XAPK/APKS), and heavily protected or obfuscated APKs may be rejected. Nothing is returned unless the result passes verification.

      Only use APKs you are allowed to modify.

      Use this on your own apps or apps whose license permits modification. Don't redistribute modified apps you don't own.

      Compatibility is never guaranteed. Always test the modified app.

      Privacy: uploaded and generated files are stored privately and deleted automatically after the retention period. APKs are never executed on the server.
